SPINSMITH: PRIVACY

SpinSmith collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no network calls of its own after it loads.

Your progress (smith level, unlocked parts, your top, your arenas, settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage on your headset. Clearing the site's data, or adding ?fresh to the address, removes it.

Hand tracking and your room. The game asks the browser for hand tracking and for detected planes (the table it stands on). This data is used live, on the headset, to play; it is never stored or sent anywhere.
