# SpinSmith: testing notes

Draft (Oct 8, 2026); final text before the deadline. In English, as the rules require. For judges and anyone trying the build.

## Before you start

0. **Mark your desk (and a wall) in the Quest's Space Setup** (Settings > Physical space >
   Space setup): label the desk as a table or desk, and the wall behind it as a wall. The arena
   then stands on your real desk, thrown tops can skate off its real edge, and rivals arrive
   through a portal on the wall. Without these labels the game still plays fully, on a floating
   tray in front of you, and says so.
1. **Sit at a table.** SpinSmith is seated: the arena stands on the table in front of you.
2. **Hands only.** In Settings, turn on hand tracking and put the controllers down. Every action
   is a pinch, a pull or a fingertip press.
3. **Allow spatial data** when the browser asks. The arena then stands on your real table;
   without it, or with no table nearby, it floats on a tray in front of you and plays the same.
4. Open the link and press **Enter**. Look at the table and pinch, or lay your palm on the glowing
   ring: the arena rises there at the table's real height. After 8 seconds it places itself. On a
   later visit, a persistent spatial anchor restores the smithy where you left it when supported.

## If something is off

- **The arena is in the wrong place:** at home, pinch the brass handle on the front of the pedestal
  and carry the arena where you want it; the new pose is saved. Recenter only if the entire room
  coordinate system is wrong.
- **Someone played before you on this headset:** add `?fresh` to the address to start as a new
  smith, or hold your level plate (the small plate in front of the arena) for 2 seconds and pinch
  it again.
- **To see later progression right away:** add `?demo` for a level-8 smith with order stamps,
  rival marks and champions.

## The first five minutes

1. Your top waits in its dock on the left of the arena. Pinch it and drop it in the arena.
2. The rival is introduced. Pinch over the arena where you want to launch (inside the dashed
   ring), pull back and let go: the top rips
   in. A white-hot pull adds spin.
3. Round one: a ring marks a spot beside the rival. Pinch the floor there and a ramp rises at once;
   drag to aim it. Your ramp throws the rival toward the rim.
4. Round two: press a fingertip into the floor ahead of a top and a cup opens: it catches the
   first top to roll in. Pinch the caught top, pull back and let go: it flies the other way
   (aimed near a slag pit, it goes straight in). An empty or unused cup lets go after 2 seconds.
5. Round three: the glowing ring on the back of your hand is the forge bar. It begins nearly full;
   when ready, slap your palm on the table beside the arena (a glowing handprint shows where): the
   Anvil Strike raises two ridges and throws the tops near the rim. In this opening lesson every
   finish scores one heat, so all three hand moves get their own round. Later knockouts score two.
   After the lesson, rivals flash and whine before their big moves: strike first to cut one short.
   `?demo` has all of this open at once.
6. First to 3 heat wins: outlasting the rival scores 1, a knockout (into a slag pit, over the
   wall, or shattering it) scores 2. A finish your ramp or sling caused says so: YOUR RAMP → SLAG.
7. After the first match the small anvil calls: pinch it and the arena turns into the forge. The Tip
   drawer is open with your new part pulsing; pinch it, bring it to your top, let go. Drop your top
   in the glowing slot on the right to save it.
8. Back home, the Smith's board shows today's order, the rivals' medallions and your ledger.

## Moments worth seeing (about ten minutes)

- **Your room joins in.** Sit near the table's edge and ramp the rival over the wall: it keeps
  spinning across your real desk and can drop off its real edge (OFF THE DESK!). With a wall
  detected behind the arena, the rival's portal opens on it at the start of a match.
- **Strike the desk.** With the forge bar full, slap your palm beside the arena: the shockwave,
  and a molten print of your hand on the desk that cools to a scorch. Each seal also leaves its
  own persistent table mark, and the newest eight return with the workshop.
- **Replay.** When your own move decides a round, the arena replays it slowly and turns to show
  it from the rival's side (a pinch skips it).
- **Quench.** Save a top in the forge's quench slot: the room turns cold blue for a breath.
- **Win a match.** A wave of warm light rolls across your room.
- **Challenge a friend.** The board shows your top's code; on another headset, add `?vs=CODE` to
  the address and the next match is against your top.
- `?demo` opens all of this at once (level 8, every move taught).

## Known limits

- Without a table labelled in the Quest's Space Setup, the arena floats in front of you after
  8 seconds and says so; press your palm on the table to place it there instead.
- The brass dial beside your dock is the comfort dial: sound, hit flashes, and a gentle strike
  (press and hold instead of a slap).
- On a small table the arena moves in from the edge by at most 15 cm; if it still hangs over,
  pull it back with the handle.
- Rivals from level 6 use ramps too; you meet them only after some play, or at once with `?demo`
  (they are not in the first session).
- All sound is synthesized; there are no music files.
